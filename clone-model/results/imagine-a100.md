# Donk model self-trained on imagined play: passed its rule on test-C

Run on 2026-11-15 on an NVIDIA A100 (40 GB) in Google Colab, 47.5 minutes in all. Code:
`clone-model/imagine.py` at commit `0eab66c`. Design and pass rule: `clone-model/ROUND3_DESIGN.md`
8.5 and 8.3. Numbers: `imagine-a100.json`.

## What ran

Four rounds. In each round, CS-NET's own engine imagined 8 four-second futures from 200 moments of
donk's training rounds, using the current donk model. Futures that broke a human per-step limit were
thrown away; of the rest, the one closest to what donk really did was kept (190 of 200 moments gave
one). The model then trained for 100 steps on the kept futures, in the situations its own play had
created, plus donk's real windows, with the KL anchor to CS-NET. Each round was scored by free-running
play on 80 dev-A moments.

## What it means

1. **First learned donk model that passes a rule fixed before the run.** Its own generated paths are
   closer to donk's than the generic model's, it walks backwards about as often as donk, and it almost
   never breaks a human limit.
2. **Learning inside its own imagined play fixed the first model's failure.** The first donk model,
   trained only on recorded moves, was 4.5% worse than the generic model; this one is 11.3% better.
3. **View turning got worse, and got worse each round.** The judge chose futures by where donk went,
   not where he looked. The model's turn distribution moved away from donk's (4.94 against 1.32). For a
   viewer, aim matters. The next judge must also score view turning.
4. **Less variety.** The best of its 8 futures is slightly worse than the generic model's best (94.0
   against 90.1): keeping the best future each time makes the model more certain. Mean error improves
   partly because its futures spread less.

## Not done

Movement and view only, generated by CS-NET's engine: not in CS2, no combat, no people panel. test-C
is 7 rounds of one player's training matches; CS-NET itself may have been trained on these matches,
which affects the generic model and the donk model alike. The adapter (`imagined_lora.pt`, about
5 MB) is on the Colab machine only. test-C is now used.
