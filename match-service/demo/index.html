Demo script for the judges

  1. Bot vs bot. On Bot vs bot, start donk (CT) vs broky (T). Point at the two panels: each follows one bot live.
  2. Replay. Open Replay on that match, pick a round and donk: his bot's round plays next to the real donk's closest recorded round.
  3. Clone vs clone. Same page, second tab: both bots' routes in one round.
  4. Proof. Proof: how alike donk is to himself, and what the clone carries.
  5. Play. A judge sits at the CS2 PC: Play vs a bot, CT against broky.
